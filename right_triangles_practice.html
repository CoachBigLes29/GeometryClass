Problem 3
A 20 foot ramp is used at the loading dock of a factory. If the base of the ramp is placed 19 feet from the base of the dock, how high is the loading dock? Round your answer to the nearest tenth.

📚 Solution:

Using the Pythagorean Theorem:

  1. Ramp (hypotenuse) = 20 feet
  2. Base (one leg) = 19 feet
  3. Height (other leg) = h
  4. 19² + h² = 20²
  5. 361 + h² = 400
  6. h² = 39
  7. h = √39 ≈ 6.2 feet

Problem 5
Find y given the following triangle. Leave your answer in radical form. No decimals.
Isosceles right triangle with two equal sides of 8 and base y

📚 Solution:

This is an isosceles right triangle (45-45-90 triangle):

  1. Two equal legs = 8
  2. Hypotenuse = y
  3. Using Pythagorean Theorem: 8² + 8² = y²
  4. 64 + 64 = y²
  5. 128 = y²
  6. y = √128 = √(64 × 2) = 8√2
  7. Answer: y = 8√2
Problem 6

45-45-90 Triangle Properties:

  5. Answer: x = 8√2, y = 8

Problem 10
Max built a skateboarding ramp that is 16 inches high. The angle formed by the ramp and the ground is 21°. What is the length of the ramp?

📚 Solution:

Using Trigonometry (SOH CAH TOA):

  1. Height (opposite) = 16 inches
  2. Angle = 21°
  3. Ramp (hypotenuse) = ?
  4. sin(21°) = opposite/hypotenuse
  5. sin(21°) = 16/ramp
  6. ramp = 16/sin(21°)
  7. ramp = 16/0.358 ≈ 44.6 inches
Problem 11
A wire is attached from the top of a 30 foot telephone pole to a stake in the ground. If the angle formed by the wire and the pole is 48°, what is the length of the wire?

📚 Solution:

Using Trigonometry (SOH CAH TOA):

  1. Pole height (adjacent to the 48° angle) = 30 feet
  2. Angle = 48°
  3. Wire (hypotenuse) = ?
  4. cos(48°) = adjacent/hypotenuse
  5. cos(48°) = 30/wire
  6. wire = 30/cos(48°)
  7. wire = 30/0.669 ≈ 40.3 feet

  7. Answer: x ≈ 38.6, y ≈ 52.8

Problem 17
The perimeter of a square is 36 feet. Find the length of the diagonal to the nearest tenth.

📚 Solution:

Using Pythagorean Theorem:

  1. Perimeter = 36 feet
  2. Side of square = 36 ÷ 4 = 9 feet
  3. The diagonal forms a right triangle with two sides
  4. d² = 9² + 9²
  5. d² = 81 + 81 = 162
  6. d = √162 = 9√2
  7. d ≈ 12.7 feet
