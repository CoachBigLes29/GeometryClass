TSI Math · Percent Discount

A backpack costs $60. It’s on sale for 25% off. What is the sale price before tax?

Show hint

Find the amount taken off the original price.

Show worked solution

25% = ¼. Divide $60 by 4 to get a $15 discount. Then subtract $15 from $60 to get a sale price of $45.

Discuss What does the $15 represent? What does the $45 represent?

Simple Probability

A bag contains 4 red, 3 blue, and 5 green marbles. If you draw one at random, what is the probability of drawing blue?

Show hint

Count the blue marbles, then count all the marbles.

Show worked solution

There are 3 blue marbles and 4 + 3 + 5 = 12 total. Divide 3 by 12 to get ¼, or 25%.

Discuss Why is 12 the denominator?

SOH CAH TOA

Find sin A. Give your answer as a fraction.

Show hint

Use SOH. Sine is opposite ÷ hypotenuse. Which side is directly across from angle A?

Show worked solution

From angle A, BC is the opposite side (32). AC is the hypotenuse (40) because it is across from the right angle at B. So sin A = 32/40 = 4/5.

Discuss Which side is adjacent to angle A, and why?
